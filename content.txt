Headline: Aleksandr® / Graphic design
About:
I make "graphic design."
Sometimes it's a campaign. Sometimes it's a brand built from zero: the logo, the mascot, the merch, the poster, the game that drops on launch day. Sometimes it's the system that lets ten people do all of that faster. I don't separate those. Same practice, different outputs.
I work like a tourist in every discipline. Curious, a little naive, never too precious. Purists know the rules too well to break them. I'd rather walk in with fresh eyes, touch everything, and leave something that wasn't there before.
My tools are whatever gets the idea out of my head fastest. AI, a pen, a plugin I wrote at 2 a.m. because I refused to do the same thing by hand one more time. The tool is never the point. The edit is.
I design for the thumb mid-scroll and the person walking past a wall. If either one stops for half a second, the job's done. If they screenshot it, even better.
Still a student. Always will be.
Based in Yerevan. Open to relocation.
